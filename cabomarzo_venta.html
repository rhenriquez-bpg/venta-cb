Property Description

Welcome to PH Cabomarzo, a prestigious high-floor residence in the heart of San Francisco and Coco del Mar, Panama's most coveted neighborhoods. This exceptional penthouse offers breathtaking, unobstructed ocean views and views of cargo ships awaiting entry to the Panama Canal.

With a generous 173m² of premium living space on a high floor, this property captures the essence of urban luxury. Located in an exclusive two-units-per-floor building, enjoy privacy and sophistication with direct marina and ocean vistas from multiple rooms.

This is the perfect choice for those seeking upscale city living combined with waterfront serenity and the unique perspective of international maritime activity.
